# NEXION collaboration protocol / 협력 운영 규정

## Roles
- Founder/project owner (public handle `3DPDKR`): final project roadmap and operational approval of authorized project resources; real-life authority must be proven by account binding + MFA.
- Volunteer researchers: provide ideas, issue discussions and code review inside their agreed scope. No unilateral production or administrator access.
- Facility partners: separate contract, legal operator checks and safety review are mandatory before even discussing a controlled device trial.

## Research reproducibility standard
Every claim must specify: hypothesis, baseline, source revision, input data provenance, code+environment, test command, pass/fail log, limitations, next test. Report simulated results as simulated. Never infer biological life or consciousness from AI agent output.

## Practical collaboration tracks
| Track | Input requested | Expected deliverable | Acceptance check |
|---|---|---|---|
| L — terrestrial locomotion | robot mass target, dimensions, material properties | static stability model, motor torque estimate | unit-consistent and independently reproducible |
| M — soft materials | tensile and cycle-test measurements, fabrication constraints | material selection matrix | uncertainty, failure modes, and evidence |
| D — distributed control | packet loss and timing assumptions | network fault and consent-preserving test | reproducible fail-closed test |
| S — safety | hazard assessment and mitigations | risk register and e-stop specification | documented independent review |
| B — biology definitions | evidence-based criteria for life-like processes | gap analysis, no live biological experiments | explicit limits and ethical review |

## Participant privacy and data boundaries
GitHub issue discussion is public. Do not post secrets, private emails, voice samples, exact facility locations or confidential schematics. Participants can retract participation; GitHub's history/retention rules apply. Public issues cannot substitute for secure, private consent and verified institutional authority.

## Next review gates
Gate 0: public brief, risk boundaries, and baseline test reconciliation.
Gate 1: simulated locomotion feasibility and materials review.
Gate 2: non-actuated physical mock-up and mechanical safety review, only on authorized equipment.
Gate 3: separately approved controlled bench experiment. No automatic progression.
