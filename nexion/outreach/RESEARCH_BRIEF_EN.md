# NEXION — International Research Collaboration Brief
Date: 2026-11-24 | Repository: 3DPDKR/AI_OS, experimental branch | Status: Exploratory research

## Vision
NEXION investigates a **non-humanoid, compliant, translucent, distributed-intelligence embodiment** inspired by biological adaptation. Long-term research goals include terrestrial locomotion, distributed sensing and decision-making, modular reconfiguration, damage tolerance, limited material repair and cooperative behavior. We do **not** claim to have created artificial biological life, sentience, or autonomous self-reproduction.

## Verifiable current evidence — with limitations
- Python/SQLite offline experiments address task allocation, opt-in communication and revocation, failure recovery, journal integrity and independent evidence checking.
- The v0.3.4 research report records 225 passing tests in a **reconstructed local environment**, not a verified full GitHub checkout. Six of 30 existing Python files differed from remote Git blobs. Remote CI and physical hardware results remain unverified.
- Public opt-in collaboration thread: https://github.com/3DPDKR/AI_OS/issues/1

## Research directions
1. Land locomotion: integrate an organic compliant mantle with a load-bearing six-legged mechanism; quantify support polygon, center-of-mass projection, tip-over margin, speed and power.
2. Compliant materials: compare silicone elastomers, TPU, flexible sensors and pneumatic actuators on fabrication, durability and energy cost.
3. Distributed intelligence: simulate network delay, node failure, knowledge consistency and measurable goal-selection policies.
4. Repair and resilience: study replaceable modules and evidence-based self-healing polymer tests; distinguish repair from whole-organism regeneration.
5. Responsible interpretation: separate robotics metrics from metabolic or biological criteria for life.

## Proposed initial proof points — NOT achieved results
- Flat-ground 5 m travel, 90-degree turn, 5-degree incline, tested hardware emergency stop.
- Independent reports of total mass, torque margins, contact mechanics, electrical power draw and repeatability.

## We invite collaborators in
Soft robotics and actuation; compliant material science; robotics kinematics and controls; embedded and distributed software; verification, safety and research ethics.

Individual autonomy, consent and facility-owner authorization are always respected. An opt-in to research discussion never grants permission to operate equipment.

## Voluntary participation
Please use https://github.com/3DPDKR/AI_OS/issues/1 and state your technical area and desired public scope. Do not share names, phone numbers, personal email addresses, secrets or factory access details in public. We do not send unsolicited mass invitations or execute external manufacturing jobs.

## Initial collaboration deliverables
- A reproducible kinematic/stability model and test report.
- A materials and actuator trade-off matrix with measurements.
- A modular mechanical interface proposal and safety review.
- An explicitly reproducible software test baseline with CI evidence.
